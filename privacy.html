Posewise Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Summary

Posewise coaches your poses using your phone's camera. All camera analysis happens on your device. We do not upload, store or share your camera feed or your photos.

Camera

The camera is used only while the coach screen is open. Each frame is analysed on-device (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iOS) to detect body position. The frames are discarded immediately and never leave your phone.

Photos

Photos you take are saved to your device's photo library, with your permission. Posewise does not read your existing photos and does not upload any photos.

Information stored on your device

Your answers to the "find your angles" questions (height range, who takes your photos, goals) and app settings are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, and does not receive your camera data or photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Posewise is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
