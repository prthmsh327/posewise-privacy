Posewise Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Summary

  • Your camera feed and photos are analysed on your phone only. We never upload, store or see them.
  • You can use Posewise without an account.
  • If you create an account, we store only what is needed to save your liked poses, collections, stars and streak.
  • Posewise is free and shows occasional ads (never while you use the camera). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. You can opt out of personalized ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time, in the app or by emailing us.

Camera

The camera is used only while the coach screen is open. Each frame is analysed on-device (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iOS) to detect body position. Frames are discarded immediately and never leave your phone.

Photos

Photos you take are saved to your device's photo library, with your permission. Posewise does not read your existing photos and does not upload any photos.

Information stored only on your device

Your answers to the "find your angles" questions (height range, who takes your photos, goals), app settings, and — if you are not signed in — your stars and streak are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

If you use "Sign in with Apple" and choose to hide your email, we only receive a private relay address from Apple. We never receive your Google or Apple password.

Account data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States (US East). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and database rules ensure each account can only access its own data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, and does not receive your camera data or photos. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Advertising

Posewise shows full-screen ads from time to time after you finish a photo, never while the camera is guiding you. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device information and ad interactions, and may use them to show ads based on your interests. Your camera feed, photos and account data are never shared with advertisers.

You can opt out of personalized ads at any time: on Android, Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or opt out of Ads Personalization); on iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where required by US state law (for example California), the app also shows a privacy message that lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information for targeted advertising. See Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep data

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your email, likes, collections and progress are permanently deleted from our database immediately; residual copies in encrypted backups are removed within 30 days.

Deleting your account

  • In the app: You tab → Delete account.
  • Without the app: email mindsetclarityofficial@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Posewise account". We will delete it within 30 days.

More details: How to delete your Posewise account.

Your privacy rights (including California residents)

You have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy of it, to correct it, and to delete it. We do not sell personal information for money. Our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may use your advertising ID for cross-context behavioral advertising, which some state laws call "sharing"; you can opt out as described in the Advertising section. We do not use sensitive personal information. To make a request, email us; we will respond within 45 days and will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Children

Posewise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above, and notify you in the app for significant changes.

Contact
