Delete your Posewise account

Posewise — photo posing coach, by Posewise

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Posewise and sign in.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

Email mindsetclarityofficial@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Posewise account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address, name and account ID)
  • Your liked poses and collections
  • Your synced stars and streak

Everything is deleted permanently. Nothing is kept, except residual copies in encrypted backups, which are removed within 30 days. Photos you took were never uploaded, so they stay in your phone's gallery.
